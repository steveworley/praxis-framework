What's deliberately out ideas considered and shelved scope
  • Search. Page-level filters cover roles up to ~100 artifacts in any one category. Add later if a role outgrows that.
  • Multi-role view. Praxis is one repo per role; multi-role = multiple clones. No role-switcher in the UI.
  • Rule-violation detection. Auto-comparing actual behavior against persona inhibitions is hard and error-prone. Defer.
  • A separate /proposed page. Drafts surface in escalations (where review happens) and in /role (where the supervisor sees the role's evolving capability set). No third surface.
  • Charts / dashboards-of-charts. The summary cards on home are textual, not graphical. Praxis is reading-shaped, not BI-shaped.
